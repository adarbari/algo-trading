### `rollup.analyst_ratings@v1.rating_strong_buy`

**How to read it.** How many analysts rate the stock a strong buy in the newest month the vendor lists. A count of 10 is ten analysts. Compare it with rating_count for the share, not the count alone.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| several analysts rate it a strong buy | `gte 3` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Analysts rate on different dates, and the vendor does not date each rating. A count mixes views from last week and from a year ago.
- A few analysts make a thin consensus. Read rollup.analyst_ratings@v1.rating_count beside the counts.
- Ratings follow the price: analysts upgrade after a rise and downgrade after a fall. A strong rating can be the lag of a rally.
- Null with ratings_status NOT_COVERED or NOT_FETCHED reads as not covered by our analyst data. The vendor does not answer for every name, and the nightly rotation has not asked every name yet.
- Null with ratings_status STALE: the newest snapshot is over 45 days old, so no value is served.

Sources: Finnhub analyst recommendation trends, stored nightly in analyst/estimate_snapshots (docs/adr/0068-analyst-data-vendors.md)

### `rollup.analyst_ratings@v1.rating_score`

**How to read it.** The mean analyst rating on a scale of 1 to 5. A 5 is every analyst at strong buy, 3 is hold, 1 is strong sell. Most covered stocks sit between 3.5 and 4.5, since analysts seldom publish a sell.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| most analysts rate it a buy | `gte 4.0` | hard | - | - |
| analysts neutral or better | `gte 3.0` | soft | 0.3 | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Analysts lean to buy, so a score of 3.0 is already a cautious consensus. Compare the score with other names, not with the midpoint of the scale.
- A score from a handful of analysts moves with each of them. Read rollup.analyst_ratings@v1.rating_count beside it.
- The score says what analysts rate, not what the stock will do. A high score after a rally is often the lag of the price.
- Null when no analyst rates the name, and with ratings_status STALE, NOT_COVERED or NOT_FETCHED. A hard criterion rejects those names.

Sources: Finnhub analyst recommendation trends, stored nightly in analyst/estimate_snapshots (docs/adr/0068-analyst-data-vendors.md)

### `rollup.analyst_ratings@v1.ratings_status`

**How to read it.** Whether the analyst ratings of the name can be used. OK: a snapshot at most 45 days old. STALE: older, so no value is served. NOT_COVERED: the vendor has no ratings for the name. NOT_FETCHED: we have not asked yet.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ratings are current | `eq "OK"` | hard | - | - |
| leave out stale snapshots | `ne "STALE"` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- NOT_COVERED and NOT_FETCHED read the same to a trader, as not covered. The admin cause tells them apart, and NOT_FETCHED ends as the nightly rotation reaches the name.
- STALE is not the old value: a snapshot past 45 days serves nothing, so a vendor outage shows as STALE rather than as old counts.
- OK does not say the score has a 90-day change: rollup.analyst_ratings@v1.rating_score_change_90d is null until our history is that long.

Sources: Finnhub analyst recommendation trends, stored nightly in analyst/estimate_snapshots (docs/adr/0068-analyst-data-vendors.md)

### `rollup.analyst_revisions@v1.eps_fy1_revision_30d`

**How to read it.** How much the consensus EPS for the current fiscal year moved in 30 days, in percent of the old estimate, for the same year. A value of +5 means analysts raised it by 5%. -10 means a 10% cut.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| analysts raising this year's EPS estimate | `gte 2` | hard | - | with rollup.analyst_revisions@v1.revisions_status eq OK and rollup.analyst_estimates@v1.eps_fy1_analysts gte 5 |

**When the reading lies**

- Our history starts on 2026-10-19, so this is null until about 2026-11-18. Read rollup.analyst_revisions@v1.revisions_status for whether the windows exist yet.
- The comparison is for the same fiscal year, matched on its year end. At the year roll a year the old snapshots never listed has no past value, so the field reads null, not a jump.
- A small old estimate makes a big percentage: the field is null when the old EPS is under five cents in size. Read it beside rollup.analyst_estimates@v1.eps_fy1.
- One analyst moving a thin estimate shifts it a lot. Read rollup.analyst_estimates@v1.eps_fy1_analysts, and prefer 5 or more.
- A name with no row has no current estimates: not covered, never asked, or the newest snapshot is STALE. A hard criterion rejects it.

### `rollup.analyst_revisions@v1.revisions_status`

**How to read it.** Whether the estimate revisions of the name can be measured. OK: a snapshot of the same fiscal year exists for 30, 60 and 90 days back. BUILDING: an earlier one exists, but not for every window. NO_PRIOR: none holds this year.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| only names whose revisions over every window can be measured | `eq "OK"` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- OK says the comparisons exist, not that the estimate moved: most names read a revision near 0. Read the revision fields for the size.
- BUILDING lasts until 2027-01-17 for every name, since our history starts on 2026-10-19. A night the rotation missed can also hold a covered name there.
- NO_PRIOR also covers a year that the older snapshots never listed, as at the fiscal year roll, and a name whose first estimate year is unknown.
- A name with no row has no current estimates: not covered, never asked, or the newest snapshot is STALE. A hard criterion rejects it.

Sources: Financial Modeling Prep analyst estimates, stored nightly in analyst/estimate_snapshots (docs/adr/0068-analyst-data-vendors.md)
