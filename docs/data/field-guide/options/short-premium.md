### `rollup.short_premium@v1.premium_status`

**How to read it.** Whether both the put and the call at about 0.20 delta were found. OK means both. Otherwise it names the first failing step. NO_SPOT: no usable price for the underlying. NO_CHAIN: no option quotes. NO_EXPIRY: no listed expiry within 30% of 30 days. ILLIQUID: a side has no two-sided quote at the expiry, or none whose volatility we could solve. NO_STRIKE: no strike within 0.025 delta of 0.20 on a side.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| both sides found | `eq "OK"` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- A side that was found keeps its figures when the other side fails: the put columns can be filled while the status says ILLIQUID for the calls. Read the status before treating a null as 'no premium'.
- NO_STRIKE on a liquid name usually means a wide strike ladder (a high-priced stock) that skips the 0.20 delta band, not a thin market.

### `rollup.short_premium@v1.target_expiry`

**How to read it.** The expiry the summary uses: the listed expiry nearest 30 calendar days away, within 30% of it (21 to 39 days), ties to the earlier. It is the grid's 30-day column.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| has a 30-day expiry | `not_null` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Weekly expiries count, so it can sit nearer 30 days than the standard monthly. Different from rollup.put_wing@v1.target_expiry (near 45 days among 30 to 60).

Sources: docs/options/backend-concepts-2.md

### `rollup.short_premium@v1.target_dte`

**How to read it.** Calendar days from the session to rollup.short_premium@v1.target_expiry: 21 to 39, nearest 30. The yield a year divides by it.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| close to 30 days | `between [25, 35]` | soft | 4 | - |

**When the reading lies**

- A name with few listed expiries can sit at either edge of the window; compare yields only when the days are close.

### `rollup.short_premium@v1.put_strike`

**How to read it.** The strike of the put to sell: the two-sided put whose delta is nearest minus 0.20, within 0.025 of it. The delta is solved from the put's mid with the Treasury rate and the dividend yield. Its cash collateral is this strike times 100 per contract.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| has a 0.20 delta put | `not_null` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- The delta is ours, not the feed's. The strike is a pick from the stored (end-of-day) quote: judge the trade on a live one.

Sources: docs/options/backend-concepts-2.md

### `rollup.short_premium@v1.put_breakeven`

**How to read it.** The price the underlying can fall to at the expiry before the sold put loses money: the strike minus the mid premium.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| has a put breakeven | `not_null` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Ignores commissions, early assignment and any move in the price before the expiry; it is the expiry payoff, not a stop.

Sources: docs/options/backend-concepts-2.md

### `rollup.short_premium@v1.put_yield_ann`

**How to read it.** The put's mid premium over its strike, scaled to a year by 365 over the days to the expiry. It is simple, not compounded: what the cash-secured put would pay a year if it repeated at this price. 0.15 is 15% a year.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a put yielding at least 10% a year | `gte 0.1` | soft | 0.02 | - |

**When the reading lies**

- It is a rate of premium, not a forecast of profit: a put that is assigned loses what the stock falls below the breakeven. A high yield is usually a high implied volatility paying for a real risk.
- Short expiries and wide spreads inflate it: read rollup.short_premium@v1.target_dte and the spread on the grid before comparing.

Sources: docs/options/backend-concepts-2.md

### `rollup.short_premium@v1.call_strike`

**How to read it.** The strike of the call to sell against shares held: the two-sided call whose delta (ours) is nearest 0.20, within 0.025 of it.

**The criterion per intent**

|---|---|---|---|---|

### `rollup.short_premium@v1.call_breakeven`

**How to read it.** The price the shares can fall to at the expiry before the covered call loses on the stock: the price now minus the mid premium.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| has a call breakeven | `not_null` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Shares called away above the strike give up the move beyond it; the breakeven says nothing about that cost.

### `rollup.short_premium@v1.call_yield_ann`

**How to read it.** The call's mid premium over the price of the shares held, scaled to a year by 365 over the days to the expiry. It is simple, not compounded: the income a covered call would add a year if it repeated at this price.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a call yielding at least 8% a year | `gte 0.08` | soft | 0.02 | - |

**When the reading lies**

- It excludes the stock's own move and the dividend. A high yield pays for the risk of the shares being called away on a rally, or of an event before the expiry.
