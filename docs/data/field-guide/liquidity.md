# Field guide: liquidity

### `rollup.price_stats@v2.adv_usd_20d`

**How to read it.** Average dollars traded a day over 20 sessions (close times volume). The usual sizing rule is to hold no more than 1% to 2% of a day's volume, so the threshold follows from the position: a $50,000 position wants about $5M a day, $500,000 wants $50M. $5M is 'some volume', $50M is liquid, $100M and above is very liquid; under $1M a day is where spreads and slippage dominate any edge.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| liquid (a $500k position) | `gte 50000000` | soft | 0.2 x the threshold, LIQUIDITY_RISK | 'very liquid' 100000000; 'some volume' 5000000; or feature.liquidity_class in [HIGH, MEDIUM] |
| tradable at all (a small account) | `gte 5000000` | soft | 0.2 x the threshold, LIQUIDITY_RISK | - |

**When the reading lies**

- An ETF's on-screen volume understates its liquidity: market makers create and redeem shares, so a fund with thin volume can still absorb a large order if its holdings are liquid. For funds (instrument.is_etf eq true) a low ADV is not disqualifying by itself.
- One event day (earnings, an index inclusion) lifts a 20-day average for 20 sessions; a name just over a threshold may be thinner than it looks. Check rollup.momentum@v1.rel_volume history through rollup.price_moves@v1.one_day_move.
- Dollar volume, not shares: a $2 stock trading 10M shares is $20M a day, the same as a $200 stock trading 100k shares, and the $2 one has a far wider spread. Pair with rollup.price_stats@v2.close.

Sources: The 1% to 2% of ADV sizing rule: https://daytradingtoolkit.com/dictionary/liquidity; Position-level liquidity (days to exit, share of daily volume): https://www.alphatheory.com/blog/managing-portfolio-liquidity-position-level-calculation

### `rollup.price_stats@v2.close`

**How to read it.** The session's closing price in dollars. Under $5 is 'penny' territory (many brokers and funds exclude it; the tick is a large share of the price); under $10 spreads and ATR as a share of price are elevated; a price floor is almost always a hard gate.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a price floor | `gt 10` | hard | - | 5 for a looser floor; 'penny stocks' is close lt 5 |

**When the reading lies**

- A high price is not 'expensive' and a low price is not 'cheap'; valuation is feature.pe_ratio and size is feature.market_cap.
- Split-adjusted history: a stock that reverse-split 1:10 last month shows a continuous series and a 'high' price with the same thin market it had before.

Sources: Broker and index conventions (the $5 penny-stock line)

### `feature.liquidity_class`

**How to read it.** HIGH when every HIGH threshold holds ($100M a day, close at least $10, option tier A, chain open interest 50k and volume 5k), MEDIUM when every MEDIUM one does ($10M, $5, tier A or B, open interest 5k), else LOW; UNKNOWN when an input is missing. A composite for stock plus options liquidity in one word.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| liquid stock and options | `in ["HIGH", "MEDIUM"]` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- It bundles option liquidity: a very liquid stock with no options is LOW. For a stock-only screen use rollup.price_stats@v2.adv_usd_20d instead.
- UNKNOWN, not LOW, when the chain run did not cover the session; a hard 'in [HIGH, MEDIUM]' rejects those names on a day the chains failed.

Sources: Site thresholds (config/site/features/liquidity.toml)

### `feature.option_tier`

**How to read it.** The worse of the put and call liquidity tiers at the target expiry, A (tight, deep) to D (no usable options). A and B are tradable for a retail account; C fills badly; D has no usable series. The tiers come from the near-the-money spread as a share of the mid, open interest and volume.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tradable options | `in ["A", "B"]` | hard | - | add instrument.optionable eq true; A alone for 'tight spreads' |

**When the reading lies**

- Graded at the target expiry (30 to 45 days); a weekly or a far LEAPS series can be worse. For a specific expiry read rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_spread_pct and call_spread_pct.
- Null when the session's chain run failed for the name (STALE_DATA, FETCH_ERROR), which is not the same as D; a hard criterion rejects it that day.

Sources: Options liquidity checklist (volume, open interest, spread, size): https://www.tastylive.com/concepts-strategies/options-liquidity; Spread as a share of the premium (a $0.20 spread on a $2 option is 10% round trip): https://www.thetaedge.ai/blog/assess-liquidity-trading-options

### `feature.option_chain_oi`

**How to read it.** Open interest summed over the chain: contracts outstanding. Thousands is a thin chain, tens of thousands is tradable, hundreds of thousands and above is an index-like chain. It measures depth for exiting a position; a strike you trade can still be empty, so it is a gate, not a guarantee.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a chain with depth | `gte 10000` | soft | 0.3 x the threshold, LIQUIDITY_RISK | 50000 for 'deep'; the site's HIGH class uses 50000, MEDIUM 5000 |

**When the reading lies**

- 0 when the session's chain run did not list the name (no options), null when the run failed; the two differ.
- Open interest piles up at a few strikes and expiries; the chain total says little about the strike you want. Check rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_strike_oi for the target strike.

Sources: https://www.tastylive.com/concepts-strategies/options-liquidity

### `feature.option_chain_volume`

**How to read it.** Contracts traded across the chain on the session. Hundreds is quiet, a few thousand is active, tens of thousands is heavy. Volume is one day's activity, open interest the standing depth; both low means fills at the market maker's price.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| active options | `gte 1000` | soft | 0.3 x the threshold, LIQUIDITY_RISK | - |

**When the reading lies**

- An earnings week or an expiry Friday multiplies option volume; check rollup.earnings@v1.days_to_earnings before reading a spike as unusual activity.
- 0 when the run did not list the name, null when it failed.

Sources: https://www.tastylive.com/concepts-strategies/options-liquidity

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_spread_pct`

**How to read it.** The bid-ask spread of the short put at the target expiry as a share of its mid, on the stored after-close quote: 0.10 means a $0.20 spread on a $2.00 option, the round-trip cost of being wrong by nothing. Under 0.10 is tight, 0.10 to 0.20 is tradable with limit orders, above 0.30 the spread eats the premium.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a tradable spread (scored) | `lt 0.15` | score | 0.15 | full points under 15% of mid, none at 30% |
| tight spreads | `lte 0.1` | soft | 0.05, LIQUIDITY_RISK | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Stored after the close, when market makers widen quotes: a wide reading can be a quiet print, a tight one can be stale. Judge a trade on a live quote (the chain's live read) and keep this one as a score, as the VRP scanner does.
- The strike is the group's own short strike, not necessarily the one you would trade; rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_spread_pct is the spread at the best put.
- Null without a two-sided quote at the target expiry (rollup.option_liquidity@v1.liq_status says why).

Sources: Spread as a share of the premium: https://www.thetaedge.ai/blog/assess-liquidity-trading-options

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_zone_oi`

**How to read it.** Open interest of the target expiry's puts with a delta between 0.15 and 0.40: the standing depth where a short put would be sold. Hundreds is thin, a few thousand is tradable, tens of thousands is deep. A chain can carry a large total (feature.option_chain_oi) with nothing in this zone.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| depth where a put would be sold | `gte 2000` | soft | 0.5 x the threshold, LIQUIDITY_RISK | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Open interest is yesterday's standing book, not today's activity; pair with rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_volume for whether anyone trades it.
- Null without a target expiry or a standard series (rollup.option_liquidity@v1.liq_status).

Sources: https://www.tastylive.com/concepts-strategies/options-liquidity

### `rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_oi`

**How to read it.** Open interest of the best put: the put at the target expiry nearest the 8 to 15 delta band (rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_strike and best_put_delta name it). Above 1,000 contracts is the VRP scanner's full-score level; under 100 the exit depends on one market maker.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a put with standing depth (scored) | `gt 1000` | score | 1 x the threshold | full points above 1,000 contracts, none at 0 |

**When the reading lies**

- A single strike's open interest moves with one institutional position; a round 5,000 at one strike with 50 at its neighbours is one trade, not a liquid wing. Check rollup.put_wing@v1.wing_oi for the band's total.
- Null when no put at the target expiry has our delta in 0.05 to 0.35 (rollup.put_wing@v1.wing_status NO_STRIKE).

Sources: docs/screeners/vrp-scanner.md (owner decision 2026-10-04: liquidity scored, not gated)

### `rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_volume`

**How to read it.** Contracts of the best put traded on the session. Volume is today's activity where open interest is the standing book; a few hundred is active for a single strike, above 1,000 is the VRP scanner's full-score level, 0 on a quiet day is common even on liquid names.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a put that trades (scored) | `gt 1000` | score | 1 x the threshold | full points above 1,000 contracts, none at 0 |

**When the reading lies**

- One day's volume is noisy: an expiry Friday or an earnings week multiplies it, a holiday week halves it. Read it with rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_oi, which does not reset each day.
- Null without a best put (rollup.put_wing@v1.wing_status).

Sources: https://www.tastylive.com/concepts-strategies/options-liquidity

### `rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_spread_pct`

**How to read it.** The best put's bid-ask spread as a share of its mid on the stored after-close quote: 0.15 means the spread is 15% of the premium. Under 0.15 is the VRP scanner's full-score level, 0.30 and above scores nothing; the trade itself is judged on a live quote.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a fillable spread (scored) | `lt 0.15` | score | 0.15 | full points under 15% of mid, none at 30% |

**When the reading lies**

- After-close quotes are wide by nature; the live spread during the session is what a fill costs. Score it, never gate on it (owner decision 2026-10-04).
- A cheap far-out-of-the-money put has a wide spread in percent of a tiny mid even when the dollar spread is a nickel; read rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_mid with it.
- Null without a best put (rollup.put_wing@v1.wing_status).

Sources: docs/screeners/vrp-scanner.md; https://www.thetaedge.ai/blog/assess-liquidity-trading-options

### `rollup.put_wing@v1.delta_band_distance`

**How to read it.** How far the best put's delta is from the 8 to 15 delta band: 0 inside it, else the distance to the nearer edge (a 20 delta put reads 0.05, a 5 delta put 0.03). The VRP scanner scores it rather than gating: a 20 delta put still scores, a 25 delta one less, and nothing is filtered on delta.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a put in the 8 to 15 delta band (scored) | `lte 0` | score | 0.2 | full points inside the band, none at 0.20 away |

**When the reading lies**

- The best put is chosen among our 0.05 to 0.35 delta puts, so the field never exceeds 0.20; a name whose only puts are nearer the money has no best put at all (null, rollup.put_wing@v1.wing_status NO_STRIKE).
- Delta is ours (ADR 0021), from the stored chain: on a name whose price moved after the close the band has moved too; confirm on a live quote.

Sources: docs/screeners/vrp-scanner.md (owner decision 2026-10-04: strike choice scored)
