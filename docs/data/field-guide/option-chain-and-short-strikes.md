# Field guide: option chain and short strikes

Generated by `make features-doc` from `config/site/field_guide/`; edit those files, not this page. How to read the guide, and the situations that fool several thresholds: [field-guide.md](../field-guide.md).

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.liq_status`

**How to read it.** Why the chain columns have values or not: OK (a target expiry and a standard series), NO_STANDARD_SERIES (only non-standard contracts, e.g. after a split), NO_TARGET_EXPIRY (no expiry 14 days out or more), or the fetch's own failure (NO_CHAIN: no options listed; STALE_DATA: the feed's snapshot was old; FETCH_ERROR; NOT_ATTEMPTED). Only OK rows carry strikes, quotes and tiers.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a usable chain this session | `eq "OK"` | hard | - | soft when a day's failed fetch should list the name rather than drop it |

**When the reading lies**

- STALE_DATA and FETCH_ERROR are about the session's pull, not the name: the same name is usually OK the next session. A hard gate on OK drops it for a day; feature.option_chain_known separates 'no options' from 'the run failed'.
- NO_CHAIN is the durable 'no options' answer; instrument.optionable says whether options are listed at all.

Sources: Site convention (features/rollups/options/option_liquidity.py)

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.chain_oi`

**How to read it.** Open interest summed over every standard-series contract of the name: the standing depth of the whole chain. Under 5,000 is a thin chain, 20,000 is the tier A floor, hundreds of thousands is an index-like name. feature.option_chain_oi is the same number with 0 for a name the run did not list.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a chain with depth | `gte 20000` | soft | 0.5 x the threshold, LIQUIDITY_RISK | 5000 for 'some options' (the MEDIUM class), 50000 for the HIGH class |

**When the reading lies**

- A chain total says nothing about the strike and expiry you want: open interest piles up in a few monthlies and round strikes. Read rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_zone_oi or rollup.put_wing@v1.wing_oi for the band you trade.
- LEAPS inflate the total for large caps; a name can carry 100,000 contracts a year out and 500 in the front month.
- Null when the fetch failed or there is no standard series.

Sources: https://www.tastylive.com/concepts-strategies/options-liquidity

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.chain_volume`

**How to read it.** Contracts traded on the session over every standard-series contract: the day's activity, where open interest is the standing book. Hundreds is quiet, a few thousand is active, tens of thousands is heavy; 5,000 is the HIGH liquidity class's floor.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| options that trade every day | `gte 1000` | soft | 0.5 x the threshold, LIQUIDITY_RISK | - |

**When the reading lies**

- One day's volume: an expiry Friday, an earnings week or one block trade multiplies it. Read it with rollup.option_liquidity@v1.chain_oi, which does not reset each day.
- Volume much larger than open interest (several times) on a quiet name is one trade opening and closing, or an index event, not a liquid chain.

Sources: https://www.tastylive.com/concepts-strategies/options-liquidity

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.expiries_within_60d`

**How to read it.** How many expiries are listed in the next 60 calendar days: 2 means monthlies only, 8 to 10 means weeklies, more means a name with daily or several weekly series. More expiries means finer control of days to expiry and usually a more active chain; fewer means the target expiry can be a long way from 35 days.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| weeklies listed | `gte 6` | soft | 2 | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Weeklies exist on liquid names, but the count alone is not liquidity: a thin name can list weeklies nobody trades. Pair with rollup.option_liquidity@v1.chain_oi.
- Right after a monthly expiry the count drops by one until the next series lists; a threshold on it wobbles month to month.

Sources: Site convention

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.target_expiry`

**How to read it.** The expiry the short-strike columns describe: the standard monthly nearest 35 days within 21 to 60 days out, else any expiry in that window, else any at least 14 days out. It is a date to show, not a threshold; rollup.option_liquidity@v1.target_dte is the number of days.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| has a target expiry | `not_null` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Null when no expiry is 14 days out or more (liq_status NO_TARGET_EXPIRY): a name whose options stop listing, or a chain fetched without far expiries.
- It is not the nearest expiry (rollup.nearest_expiry@v1.expiry_date) nor the put wing's 45-day target (rollup.put_wing@v1.target_expiry); three groups, three expiries.

Sources: Site convention (features/rollups/options/option_liquidity.py)

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.target_dte`

**How to read it.** Calendar days from the session to the target expiry, usually 21 to 60 and near 35 (the standard monthly). Days to expiry set how fast premium decays and how much a move hurts: 30 to 45 days is the premium-selling convention (theta is fast, gamma still moderate); under 14 days the position is mostly gamma.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a 30 to 45 day short option | `between [28, 50]` | soft | 7 | - |

**When the reading lies**

- A reading outside 21 to 60 means the fallback picked a far or near expiry because the window had none; the tier columns then describe an expiry you may not want.
- Earnings before the target expiry is EVENT_RISK: compare with rollup.earnings@v1.days_to_earnings (sessions, not calendar days).

Sources: tastylive on 45 days to expiry for premium selling; site convention

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.short_put_ok`

**How to read it.** True when the short put's tier is A or B: a put that can be sold at the target expiry with a tight enough spread, enough open interest around it and a bid worth collecting. The one-flag form of rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_tier for a short-put or cash-secured-put screen.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a sellable put | `eq true` | hard | - | or feature.option_tier in [A, B] for both sides at once |

**When the reading lies**

- Judged on the stored after-close quote; a B at the close can be an A or a C live. Confirm on the live chain before trading.
- Null when there is no target expiry or the fetch failed, which a hard criterion rejects; feature.option_tier eq D is the durable 'no usable options'.

Sources: Site convention (TierRule thresholds)

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.underlying_price`

**How to read it.** The underlying's price captured with the chain snapshot: the price the strikes, deltas and spreads were quoted against. Usually within a few cents of rollup.price_stats@v2.close; a gap between the two means the snapshot was taken at another time than the close.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a chain quoted against a price | `not_null` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- When it differs from the close by more than the day's range, the chain is from another time (rollup.option_liquidity@v1.chain_asof): deltas and 'at the money' are off by that move.
- Null when no underlying quote came with the chain.

Sources: Site convention

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.iv30`

**How to read it.** The feed's own 30-day implied volatility, in percentage points: 25.3 is 25.3% a year. The same quantity as rollup.iv30@v1.iv30_cboe (which is a fraction, 0.253); it is kept here as the chain snapshot's number. Compare with the name's history (feature.iv_rank) and realised movement (feature.iv_hv_spread) rather than against one level.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| high implied volatility (the feed's) | `gte 40` | soft | 5 | prefer feature.iv_rank gte 0.5, relative to the name's own year |

**When the reading lies**

- Percentage points, not a fraction: 'IV over 40%' is gte 40 here and gte 0.40 on rollup.iv_history@v2.iv30. Mixing the two is the commonest threshold error on this field.
- The feed's method is not ours (ADR 0021): on thin chains the two disagree by several points; feature.vrp_iv30 takes the lower of IBKR's and Cboe's for that reason.
- Every earnings and event caveat of rollup.iv_history@v2.iv30 applies.

Sources: Cboe's IV30 definition; ADR 0021 for ours

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.stock_volume`

**How to read it.** The underlying's share volume as the chain feed reported it on the session. Shares, not dollars: a $2 stock trading 10M shares is $20M a day. The screening measure of liquidity is rollup.price_stats@v2.adv_usd_20d (dollars, averaged); this one is a single day's count from the chain feed.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| traded today | `gte 500000` | soft | 0.5 x the threshold, LIQUIDITY_RISK | shares; prefer rollup.price_stats@v2.adv_usd_20d in dollars |

**When the reading lies**

- One day's volume, and from the options feed's quote rather than the bars: it can lag or lead the bar's volume by a session. Use adv_usd_20d for a gate.
- Null when no underlying quote came with the chain.

Sources: Site convention

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.chain_asof`

**How to read it.** The date the feed's chain snapshot was taken. It should be the session itself; an earlier date means the feed served a stale chain (liq_status STALE_DATA), and every quote, delta and open-interest column describes that earlier day.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a chain snapshot exists | `not_null` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- A stale chain after a big move puts every strike in the wrong place relative to spot; the chains step retries STALE_DATA names, so the next session is usually current.
- Null when no underlying quote came with the chain.

Sources: Site convention (ADR 0043: the chains step's acceptance checks)

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_tier`

**How to read it.** The short put's liquidity tier at the target expiry: A (spread at most 5% of mid and $0.03, 2,000 open interest in the 0.15 to 0.40 delta zone, 20,000 in the chain, a bid of at least $0.25), B (10% / $0.05 / 500 / 5,000 / $0.15), C (20% / $0.10 / 100 / 1,000 / $0.05), else D: not tradable. A and B fill at a fair price with a limit order; C costs real slippage; D has no market.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a sellable put | `in ["A", "B"]` | hard | - | - |
| tight put markets only | `eq "A"` | soft | 0, LIQUIDITY_RISK | a near miss here is B |

**When the reading lies**

- Graded on the stored after-close quote, when spreads are widest; a B at the close is often an A live, and a C is sometimes a B. Score it or confirm live rather than rejecting on C.
- The short strike is the group's pick (the tightest spread between 0.20 and 0.40 delta), not the 8 to 15 delta put the VRP scanner sells; that one's spread is rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_spread_pct.
- D also when there is no two-sided put at all; feature.option_tier takes the worse of put and call.

Sources: Site TierRule thresholds (features/rollups/options/option_liquidity.py); spread as a share of mid: https://www.thetaedge.ai/blog/assess-liquidity-trading-options

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_strike`

**How to read it.** The short put strike the tier describes: the put between 0.20 and 0.40 delta with the tightest relative spread (ties: higher open interest), else the two-sided quote nearest 0.30 delta. A level in dollars: compare it with the close (how far out of the money) or with support (rollup.swing_levels@v1.swing_low) rather than thresholding it.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| has a short put strike | `not_null` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- It is the liquidity-chosen strike, not a trade recommendation; the VRP scanner's candidate is rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_strike (8 to 15 delta).
- Null without a two-sided put with a delta at the target expiry.

Sources: Site convention

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_delta`

**How to read it.** The short put strike's delta as the feed quotes it, negative: -0.30 means the put gains about 30 cents per dollar the stock falls and has roughly a 30% chance of expiring in the money. Puts between -0.20 and -0.40 are the 'short strike zone' this group picks from; -0.08 to -0.15 is the VRP scanner's far wing (rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_delta, ours).

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a short put in the 20 to 40 delta zone | `between [-0.4, -0.2]` | soft | 0.05 | - |

**When the reading lies**

- The feed's delta, three places, from its own volatility; ours (ADR 0021) can differ by a few hundredths on thin quotes.
- A delta near -0.40 with a tier A means the liquid strike is close to the money: a short put there is a stock position with extra steps.

Sources: Black-Scholes delta as a rough probability of finishing in the money (Hull, Options, Futures and Other Derivatives)

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_bid`

**How to read it.** The short put's bid in dollars per share: what a seller collects at the market. Tier A requires at least $0.25, B $0.15, C $0.05; under that the premium does not pay for the spread and the commission. Multiply by 100 for the contract.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a premium worth collecting | `gte 0.25` | soft | 0.1, LIQUIDITY_RISK | - |

**When the reading lies**

- After-close bids are often pulled or widened; a $0.00 bid at the close can be $0.20 live. Confirm live.
- Premium per share says nothing about return on capital: a $0.50 bid on a $200 strike is 0.25%; rollup.put_wing@v1.best_put_roc is the ratio.

Sources: Site TierRule bid floors

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_ask`

**How to read it.** The short put's ask in dollars per share: what a buyer pays at the market. With the bid it gives the spread (rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_spread_abs) and the mid; on its own it is a level, not a threshold.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a two-sided quote | `not_null` | hard | - | - |

**When the reading lies**

- An ask far above the bid on a thin strike is a placeholder quote, not a price; the relative spread (rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_spread_pct) is the number to judge.

Sources: Site convention

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_spread_abs`

**How to read it.** Ask minus bid at the short put strike, in dollars per share: $0.05 is a penny-increment market, $0.03 or less is tier A, $0.10 is the tier C limit. The dollar spread is the cost of crossing it once; the relative spread (rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_spread_pct) says how big that is against the premium.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a tight dollar spread | `lte 0.05` | soft | 0.05, LIQUIDITY_RISK | - |

**When the reading lies**

- Nickel-increment names cannot show less than $0.05: a $0.05 spread is tight there and loose on a penny-increment name.
- After-close quotes are wider than live ones; judge the trade live.

Sources: Site TierRule spread limits

### `rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_strike_oi`

**How to read it.** Open interest at the short put strike alone: the standing contracts at exactly the strike the tier describes. Hundreds is thin, a few thousand is tradable; the tier's own floors are on the zone (rollup.option_liquidity@v1.put_zone_oi) and the chain, not this one strike.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
