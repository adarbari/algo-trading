### `rollup.etf_macro@v1.hy_oas`

**How to read it.** The ICE BofA US high-yield option-adjusted spread over Treasuries, as a decimal: 0.03 is 3 percentage points. Low is calm credit, high is stress. Only HYG's row carries it.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a credit market that is not in stress | `lt 0.06` | soft | 0.01 | with feature.credit_spread_vs_ma200 lt 0 for a tightening trend |

**When the reading lies**

- The level alone says little: a 4 percent spread is tight in a recession year and wide in a boom. Read it against its own mean in feature.credit_spread_vs_ma200.
- Null on every fund but HYG, and null when the latest observation is more than a week old.

Sources: ICE BofA US High Yield Index Option-Adjusted Spread (FRED series BAMLH0A0HYM2)

### `rollup.etf_macro@v1.hy_oas_ma200`

**How to read it.** The mean of the high-yield option-adjusted spread over the last 200 sessions, as a decimal. The long-run level the spread is read against. Only HYG's row carries it.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a high-yield spread history long enough to read a trend against | `not_null` | hard | - | an operand of feature.credit_spread_vs_ma200; screen on that field, not on this one |

**When the reading lies**

- Needs 160 of the 200 sessions to have an observation; a shorter history is null.
- A mean lags a regime change by months. After a spike it stays high long after the spread has fallen back.

Sources: ICE BofA US High Yield Index Option-Adjusted Spread (FRED series BAMLH0A0HYM2)

### `feature.credit_spread_vs_ma200`

**How to read it.** The credit spread the fund trades over its 200-session mean, minus 1: HYG reads the high-yield spread, LQD the investment-grade one. Below 0 the spread is tighter than its trend and credit is calming; above 0 it is wider and stress is building.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a credit spread tighter than its 200-session mean | `lt 0.0` | hard | - | the macro trend test of the credit edge |

**When the reading lies**

- A spread already high and falling reads below 0 while credit is still stressed. Check rollup.etf_macro@v1.hy_oas for the level.
- Null on every fund but HYG and LQD; a missing spread or mean never passes a hard criterion.

Sources: Jostova, Nikolova, Philipov and Stahel, Momentum in Corporate Bond Returns (2013)

### `rollup.etf_macro@v1.t10y3m`

**How to read it.** The 10-year Treasury yield minus the 3-month yield, as a decimal: 0.01 is 1 percentage point. Above 0 the curve slopes up; below 0 it is inverted, a recession warning. IEF and TLT carry it.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| an upward-sloping curve | `gt 0.0` | hard | - | with rollup.trend_stats@v2.ret_252d gt 0 for the duration screen |

**When the reading lies**

- The slope alone has not timed bonds out of sample. Pair it with the fund's own trend, as in rollup.trend_stats@v2.ret_252d gt 0.
- Null on every fund but IEF and TLT, and null when the latest observation is more than a week old.

Sources: Thornton and Valente, Out-of-Sample Predictions of Bond Excess Returns and Forward Rates (2012)

### `rollup.etf_macro@v1.cape`

**How to read it.** The fund's country cyclically adjusted price to earnings ratio: the country's equity index over ten years of inflation-adjusted earnings. Low is cheap against its own history, high is expensive. Country funds and SPY carry it.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the cheapest countries by valuation | `gt 0.0` | hard | - | a presence test; rank the passing funds by this field, lowest first |

**When the reading lies**

- The history is revised in place, so a past session reads today's estimate, not what was known then. Treat a backtest on it as a valuation sort, not an as-known test.
- Cheap countries are often commodity exporters or distressed markets, so a low value mixes value with risk. Check rollup.trend_stats@v2.ret_252d.
- Null on a fund with no country series (EWM, FXI) and when the latest value is over 100 days old.

Sources: Faber, Global Value: Building Trading Models with the 10 Year CAPE (2012)

### `rollup.etf_macro@v1.fx_carry`

**How to read it.** The currency's 3-month interbank rate minus the US one, as a decimal: 0.02 means the currency yields 2 percentage points more than the dollar. Above 0 holding it pays carry; below 0 it costs carry. The currency funds carry it.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a currency that pays carry over the dollar | `gt 0.0` | hard | - | with rollup.trend_stats@v2.ret_252d for the momentum leg |

**When the reading lies**

- Only the yen, Australian dollar, Canadian dollar and Swiss franc have a fresh series. The euro and the pound are null until the registry has a rate for them.
- Carry pays until a crash: high-yield currencies fall fastest in a risk-off week. Check feature.credit_spread_vs_ma200 for stress.
- The dollar fund has no carry column. The fund's own deposit yield is not in this number.

Sources: Menkhoff, Sarno, Schmeling and Schrimpf, Carry trades and global foreign exchange volatility (2012)

### `rollup.etf_macro@v1.vx_basis`

**How to read it.** The first monthly VIX future's settle over the VIX close, minus 1. Above 0 the futures sit above spot (contango, the usual state, where a short-volatility product earns the roll); below 0 spot is above the futures (backwardation, a stress state). Only SPY's row carries it.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| futures above spot (contango) | `gt 0.0` | hard | - | the term-structure filter of a short-volatility book |

**When the reading lies**

- Needs the futures trade date to equal the VIX observation date, so a day with a stale curve is null.
- Contango is the usual state and the loss comes in the few days the curve flips. A positive basis on a calm Friday says nothing about Monday.
- Read by name through a canary on SPY, not as a field of the volatility funds: SVXY, UVXY and VIXY have no column of their own.

Sources: Simon and Campasano, The VIX Futures Basis: Evidence and Trading Strategies (2014)

### `rollup.etf_macro@v1.vx_roll`

**How to read it.** The second monthly VIX future's settle over the first, minus 1: the roll along the curve. Above 0 the curve rises with maturity (contango); below 0 it falls (backwardation). Only SPY's row carries it.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a rising futures curve (contango) | `gt 0.0` | hard | - | the second filter beside rollup.etf_macro@v1.vx_basis |

**When the reading lies**

- It reads the futures only, not the spot index, so it can stay positive while spot jumps above the front future. Check rollup.etf_macro@v1.vx_basis.
- Null with fewer than two monthly futures after the session, or a curve older than a week.

Sources: Cheng, The VIX Premium, Review of Financial Studies (2019)

### `feature.momentum_13612w`

**How to read it.** Keller's 13612W momentum score: 12 times the 1-month return, plus 4 times the 3-month, 2 times the 6-month and 1 times the 12-month return. Below 0 the fund is falling across horizons; above 0 it is rising, and the short horizons count most.

**The criterion per intent**

| Intent | Criterion | Mode | Near-miss band | How to combine it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a fund with falling momentum | `lt 0.0` | hard | - | the canary test of the Vigilant and Defensive asset allocation variants |

**When the reading lies**

- It overweights the last month, so one sharp week moves the sign. Check rollup.trend_stats@v2.ret_252d for the longer view.
- The returns are price only, with no dividends, so a bond fund can read negative while its coupon keeps it level in total return.
- Null with fewer than 252 sessions of history or a gap among them.

Sources: Keller and Keuning, Breadth Momentum and Vigilant Asset Allocation (2017)
