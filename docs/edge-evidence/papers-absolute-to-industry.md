Read from `config/site/edges/<id>/edge.toml` on 2026-10-24; sample periods checked on the web where a page states them (confidence `verified` = read from the paper's abstract page or a summary that quotes the paper's dates; `secondary` = a Quantpedia / CXO / Alpha Architect / CFA digest summary of the paper; `recalled` = memory, not checked; `unknown` = nothing found). Trial log: no edge document carries a trial log; the trial count is the official run's `trials_counted` (`src/algotrade/services/read/edge_ledger/variants.py`, `EdgeTrials.trials`, the haircut's M); the documents' `notes` only say in prose whether screeners are "ONE trial" (ensembles) or "one trial each". "Chosen after results?" quotes the notes where a choice is explained; `none stated` otherwise.

## Notes and doubts

1. **Trial log.** No `[[trials]]` table or trial count exists in any of the 40 documents; the number of trials is counted by the evaluation run (`EdgeTrials.trials` = `run.trials_counted`, `src/algotrade/services/read/edge_ledger/variants.py`) from the screens x holding periods it ran, with exactly one `official` row fixed before the frozen period was read. The notes only declare, in prose, which screeners are "ONE trial" (the ETF ensembles: absolute_momentum, bond_curve, commodity, country, credit, dual_momentum, factor, fx) and which are "one trial each" (idiosyncratic_volatility_and_max, betting_against_beta). No document says a parameter was chosen after seeing results; every threshold is stated as declared or as a data limit (e.g. dual_class "Declared thresholds, not tuned"; etf_flow "iShares only" for coverage; credit_spread's macro screener dropped because the FRED series starts 2023-10).
2. **Sample periods not confirmed on any page** (keep as `recalled`, verify before the ADR cites them): Grinblatt-Han 2005 (1963-1996); Ang et al. 2006 (1963-07 to 2000-12); Lakonishok-Shleifer-Vishny 1994 (1968-04 to 1990-04); Stambaugh-Yu-Yuan 2015 (1965-08 to 2011-01: the doc's [published] comment asserts it, the NBER/JF abstracts do not); Johnson-So 2012 O/S (1996-2010: the doc's [published] says sample = 15; only Quantpedia's own backtest 1996-2008 was found); Savor-Wilson 2016 (no years found); Johnson-So 2018 "Time Will Tell" (no years found; WSH says "more than eight years" of data); Brown-Davies-Ringgenberg 2021 (no years found; Quantpedia's working-paper backtest 2007-2015).
3. **Working-paper vs journal samples differ** for several option papers: Goyal-Saretto (WP 1996-01 to 2005-05, a secondary source says 1996-2006 for the JFE version); Cao-Han (WP 1996-01 to 2009-10); Zhan et al. (WP 1996-01 to 2012-12, RFS 2022 version unknown); Gao-Xing-Zhang (WP 1996-2010 verified, the doc's 1996-2013 for the JFQA version unverified); Ball et al. (a blog gives 1963-07 to 2013-12, the doc 1963-2014). A post-publication OOS start should use the journal version's sample end, which for these is not pinned down.
4. **Which paper is primary** is a judgement where a document cites several: absolute_momentum (Faber 2007 vs Antonacci 2013: the ensemble is neither), betting_against_beta (FP 2014 for the beta screen, Blitz-van Vliet 2007 for the vol screen), idiosyncratic_volatility_and_max (AHXZ 2006 and BCW 2011, one screener each), earnings_announcement_premium (Frazzini-Lamont 2007 remained an NBER working paper; Savor-Wilson 2016 JF covers the variant), c31 (the thesis cites Boyer-Vorkink 2014 but the measured proxy and [published] figure are BCW's MAX), c14 (Zhan et al. 2022 for the position, Cao-Han 2013 for the characteristic).
5. **Document errors found.** earnings_neutral_reversal lists So and Wang 2014 as "Journal of Financial Markets"; it is Journal of Financial Economics 114(1), 20-35. Cooper-Gulen-Schill's source url is Quantpedia, whose strategy page describes the later working paper (1968-2006, June deciles), not the JF 2008 paper (1968-2003; formation month not stated on the page). december_losers_reversal cites Grinblatt-Keloharju NBER 8745 as "on year-end selling of the year's losers": it is "Tax-Loss Trading and Wash Sales" on Finnish data, not a US return rule; Kozlowski and Lytle 2023 is marked "title not verified" in the doc itself.
6. **Pre-frozen data contamination** that affects a post-publication OOS read even where the match is close: closed_end_fund_discounts (survivor-only CEF universe before 2026-10-02; the doc says it "flatters a discount strategy"); analyst_revision, etf_flow_pressure, c04, c13, c16 (no stored history before 2026-10; the OOS window would be empty until it accrues); the ETF allocation edges use a benchmark of the book's own list, so "post-publication" there means the store's 2010 start.
7. **Decisions per year** are nominal month-end counts; the fundamentals edges (asset_growth, cash_based_operating_profitability) re-sort an annual signal monthly, so independent decisions are nearer 1-4; event edges cluster in ~4 reporting seasons; december_losers has one window a year (8 since 2018).
