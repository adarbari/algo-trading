## Notes and doubts

1. **Trial recording.** No `EdgeTrials` symbol exists in `src/algotrade`; the trial log is the run record of `edge-eval:<edge>` (ADR 0053: "every variant tried is a row of the run's trial log"; `quant/edges/statistics.py` `deflated_sharpe(x, n_trials, ...)` counts every variant, config hash and horizon). Documents state trial counts only in prose (e.g. "two trials, one screener", "three trials, not one").
2. **Post-publication OOS is already partly consumed for several papers** because our price-only history starts 2010-01-04 and outcomes reach 2018 only after the D0 Tiingo backfill: a paper whose sample ends before 2010 (JT 1993, Pontiff-Woodgate 2008, Blitz 2013 at 2008, Lakonishok-Vermaelen, McConnell-Xu, Kim-Klein-Rosenfeld, Cremers-Weinbaum, Stivers-Sun, Aboody, Chan, Da-Schaumburg, Carr-Wu, Cohen-Malloy-Pomorski) gets an OOS window that starts at our data start, not the day after the sample; the ADR should say which.
3. **Aboody, Lehavy & Trueman cut-off.** CXO's summary says the winners are "the top 1% of prior year returns"; the edge document and `[published]` call them "top decile" (+1.58% in the 5 days before). Verify in the paper before using 0.0158 as a decile figure; our top 20 by rank is closer to the top 1% of a liquid universe anyway.
4. **vix_roll_yield attribution.** The edge's "4.3 percent a month ... 2004 to 2014" comes from CXO's "Timing VIX Futures with the Futures-Spot Ratio", which summarises Cheng's "The Expected Return of Fear" (2004-03 to 2014-07), not Simon & Campasano. Simon & Campasano's rule (roll > 0.10, 5-day hold, ES hedge; Quantpedia backtest 2007-2011) is the one stated here; its own sample period is recalled, not read.
5. **treasury_month_end_extension primary paper.** The rule (hold Treasuries the last t days of the month) is Hartley & Schwarz's (WP 17-19, 1990-2018, verified); Etula et al. 2020 is the mechanism paper; the edge lists Etula first. The ADR should pick Hartley-Schwarz's sample end (2018-12) as the OOS start for this edge.
6. **Harvey, Mazzoleni & Melone** is a 2025 working paper (revised January 2026) with a sample to 2023-03; an OOS from 2023-04 holds ~12 quarter ends, and the edge tests the unconditional window, not the paper's signal.
7. **Blitz et al. sample.** Full sample 1926-2008; the abstract's large-cap claim is "post-1990"; CXO gives 1990-2008 for that subsample. Quantpedia lists 1929-2008. The edge's "1990-2009" (weekly version) is from Quantpedia's weekly variant, a different construction.
8. **Stivers & Sun.** The 2013 JBF abstract states no years; the 2010 WP's main sample is 1996-2008 (28 large caps), with 1983-2008 extended. Use 2008-12 as the sample end.
9. **option_cp_iv_signals** bundles three papers with different sample ends (2005, 2005, 2011); the ADR needs a per-screener OOS start or one document per paper. iv_change_ibkr is not any paper's rule.
10. **Unverified sample periods (recalled or unknown):** Field & Hanka (1988-1997), Pontiff & Woodgate (end year), Chan 2003 (1980-2000), Jegadeesh & Titman (1965-1989, well known), Dee et al. (unknown), Byun & Kim and Bryzgalova et al. (unknown), Brandt et al. (unknown; SSRN refused), Arnott et al. (1989-10..2017-12 from the edge), Cusatis et al. (1965-1988), Asness-Moskowitz-Pedersen (1972-2011 from the edge's page), Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen (1985-2009; Quantpedia says 1965-2009, which contradicts my memory of the paper's 58-contract sample), Moreira-Muir (1926-2015), Carr-Wu (1996-2003), Simon-Campasano (2007-2011), Chang-Hong-Liskovich (1996-2012), Etula et al. (1980-2016). Pages that refused: SSRN (403), etf.com (403), sciencedirect (403), orbilu (cert), AQR PDF pages (no years in the HTML).
11. **momentum_at_earnings is the one document whose rule was chosen after a harness result** (notes quote above); it is own, not research, whatever its citations.
12. **small_cap_earnings_drift**: "The $2B cap was decided 2026-10-08 (ED4 decisions)" is the same night the first harness runs were made; whether the cap was set before or after seeing the PEAD run is not stated.
13. **Data-start caveats common to all**: universe snapshots start 2026-10-02 (survivorship before, `pre_snapshot`); company snapshots start 2026-10-02 (CIK mapping for ipo_lockup); chains 2026-10-02; FINRA 2017-12-29; VX futures 2013; IBKR IV ~2024-10; analyst targets 2026-10-19; filing text awaits D10; 8-K/filing events await D1; outcomes to 2018 await D0 (2026-11-09) and the approved price-only backfills reach 2010-01-04.
