3. **Outcome:** The book's return over the 21 sessions from the close after each month end, less the return of 60 percent SPY and 40 percent AGG over the same sessions, net of the cost on the weight change; cash is the Treasury bill yield (ADR 0074). Price only: the stored forward return is close to close and carries no dividend or distribution, so the book and the benchmark both miss their yield and cash earns the Treasury bill.
4. **Trigger timing:** The trailing returns and hv60 are read at the month-end close D from stored bars; the book is held from the next session's close S (start_offset_sessions = 1) for 21 sessions. No fact after D enters a weight or a pick.
5. **Faithful replication:** The source's rule (Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen 2012): the sign of the trailing 12-month excess return, each position scaled to 40 percent ex-ante volatility, held a month, over 58 futures. Hurst, Ooi and Pedersen (2017) average 1, 3 and 12 months. Here: nine ETFs, long only, 3, 6 and 12 months of raw return, inverse-volatility weights, cash for a falling fund.
6. **Expected size and sample:** Hurst, Ooi and Pedersen: positive net returns in every decade since 1880 and a net Sharpe ratio near 0.4 per asset (futures, both sides); the practitioner expectation after decay for a long-only ETF version is 0.3 to 0.5. The harness's figure over the ETF history is untested here.
7. **Capacity and costs:** Capacity is large in nine liquid funds; about one switch per fund a year; a round trip costs a few basis points (6 assumed). Tax drag on the switches is the real cost and is not deducted.
8. **Failure modes and retirement:** Long-only trend is mostly equity beta in a bull market and gives up the short crisis alpha. Retire if the frozen-period picks-series t stays under 2, or the ensemble's out-of-sample Sharpe falls under the haircut figure (45 percent of the in-sample).
9. **Decoys:** Beta (a book that is mostly SPY in rising markets), a 2010s sample dominated by the US bull market, and a 1970s-style bond bear it does not contain. The benchmark mix and the decoy battery are the nulls; compare the book with SPY alone in the run record.
